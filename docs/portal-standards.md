# FarmCash Portal – Design & Commercial Standards

> These rules are enforced across the FarmCash portal.  
> Any deviation must be explicitly approved.

---

## Purpose

Defines non-negotiable design, UX, and commercial rules for the FarmCash portal.

These rules are mandatory and apply to all listings, components, and updates.

---

## 🚨 DO NOT BREAK

- All prices must display `+ VAT`
- Buyers Love section must be present
- CTA must be bottom-aligned across all cards
- Titles must be aligned across all cards
- Card structure must not change

If any of the above are altered, stop and confirm before proceeding.

---

## Pricing Rules (CRITICAL)

- All prices must display `+ VAT`
- Applies to:
  - Fixed price listings
  - Guide prices
- VAT must never be assumed or omitted

### Valid
£24,750 + VAT  

### Invalid
£24,750  
£24,750 inc VAT  

### Rationale
Prevents low-quality enquiries and ensures commercial clarity.

---

## Listing Card Structure (REQUIRED)

All listing cards must follow this structure:

1. Image  
2. Title (top-aligned)  
3. Key hook (Buyers Love or highlight)  
4. Price (+ VAT)  
5. CTA (bottom-aligned)  

### Rules

- Titles must align across all cards  
- CTAs must align at the bottom across all cards  
- Card heights should remain visually consistent  
- No layout shifting between rows  

---

## Buyers Love Section (REQUIRED)

Each listing must include a **Buyers Love** section.

### Format

- 2–4 bullet points  
- Short, benefit-led  
- Written in buyer language (not technical specs)  

### Example

- Ready to go straight to work  
- Clean, well-maintained example  
- Reliable and popular model  

### Purpose

- Drives engagement  
- Improves click-through rate  
- Supports conversion behaviour  

---

## Portal Behaviour (TEASE MODEL)

The portal page is a **conversion layer**, not a specification sheet.

### Must

- Provide enough information to encourage clicks  
- Highlight key selling points  

### Do Not

- Add full technical specifications to cards  
- Overload cards with excessive detail  

---

## Listing Identity

Each listing must include:

- Unique Listing ID (e.g. FC-XXXX)  
- Machine name (Year + Make + Model)  
- Clean, consistent formatting  

### Purpose

- Supports credibility  
- Enables easy referencing  

---

## Mobile Behaviour (PRIORITY)

- Cards must stack cleanly  
- CTA must remain visible  
- No text overflow  
- Spacing must remain consistent  

---

## Industry Alignment

The portal should follow familiar marketplace patterns (e.g. machinery and vehicle listing platforms).

### Goal

Reduce friction by aligning with expected buyer behaviour.

---

## Protected Elements

The following elements are system-critical and must not be removed or altered:

- `+ VAT` pricing display  
- Buyers Love section  
- CTA bottom alignment  
- Title alignment  
- Card structure hierarchy  
- Listing ID system  

---

## Regression Checklist (MANDATORY)

Before any update is released:

- [ ] All prices show `+ VAT`  
- [ ] Buyers Love section present  
- [ ] Titles aligned across cards  
- [ ] CTAs aligned at bottom  
- [ ] Card spacing consistent  
- [ ] No content overload on cards  
- [ ] Mobile layout verified  
- [ ] Listing ID present and correct
